## 🎯 Key Business Questions Addressed
* **Sales & Volume Trends**: How have sales amounts and order quantities trended over the last 13 weeks?
* **Customer Purchasing Habits**: Which order channels (App, Website, Instagram, Target.com, Partner App) do different gender demographics prefer?
* **Product Popularity**: Which product categories (T-Shirts, Jeans, Sneakers, etc.) drive the highest volume, and who buys them?
* **Geographic Distribution**: Where are customers located across California?
* **Fulfillment Efficiency**: How many days does it take to ship orders from purchase date?
* **Customer Satisfaction**: How are ratings distributed monthly across the quarter?

---

## 💡 Key Insights & Business Findings
* **Preferred Order Channels**: **App** is the dominant channel, with **19.0%** of total purchases made by female customers and **12.3%** by male customers.
* **Product Demand**: **T-Shirts**, **Jeans**, and **Sneakers** lead in sales volume across all customer segments.
* **Gender Demographics**: Female buyers account for **52%** of overall orders, followed by male buyers at **39%**.
* **Shipping Performance**: Average delivery time stands at **2.3 days**, with the majority of orders shipped within **1 to 2 days**.
* **Overall Rating**: Maintains a solid average customer rating of **4.0 / 5.0**.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Excel Techniques Used
* **Data Cleaning & Preparation**: `TRIM`, `UPPER`/`LOWER`, handling missing demographic values.
* **Data Modeling & Calculations**: Dynamic formulas (`SUMIFS`, `COUNTIFS`, `AVERAGEIFS`) for KPI cards.
* **Pivot Tables & Matrices**: Conditional formatting heat maps for channel preferences by gender.
* **Data Visualization**:
  * Dual-axis trend line chart (13-week performance)
  * Stacked horizontal bar chart + Donut chart overlay
  * Geographic heat map (California region distribution)
  * Frequency distribution histograms & clustered column charts
